Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

BrickFeed ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. BrickFeed is a social network for brick-building enthusiasts of all ages.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use BrickFeed, we collect and store information you provide and generate, including your display name, username, email address, bio, profile photo, background photo, posts you create, comments you write, direct messages you send, your likes and follows, reports and blocks you submit, and your app settings. We may also collect basic technical information such as device type and operating system.

How Your Information Is Stored

Your account information and content are stored using Google Firebase (including Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a cloud service provided by Google, so that your content can sync across devices and be displayed to other users of the app. Photos you add to posts and your profile are uploaded to and stored in our cloud storage so they can be displayed in the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the app: to display your profile and posts, deliver direct messages, power the leaderboard and search, and maintain community safety features. We do not sell your personal information.

Service Providers

We use Google Firebase to store and process app data; Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy. We may use limited analytics to understand app usage. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Community Safety

BrickFeed includes an always-on content filter, content reporting, and user blocking. When you report content or block a user, we store that information so we can review reports and enforce our community guidelines. We review reported content within 24 hours and remove violating content and offending users. An optional Safe Mode provides additional content filtering for users who enable it.

Children's Privacy

BrickFeed is intended for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: info@teambuddys.com
